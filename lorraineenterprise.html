About Lorraine Electronics Enterprise

Electronics & Enterprise solutions for modern businesses and individuals.

Lorraine Electronics Enterprise is a trusted provider of premium electronics and professional business solutions. We serve individuals and businesses across Kenya with quality products, expert repairs, creative services, and IT support.

Our two divisions — Lorraine Electronics and Lorraine Enterprise — work together to deliver a complete experience for our customers.

Our Mission

To provide reliable electronics, innovative technology solutions, professional repairs, IT support, and creative business services that empower individuals and organizations to work, connect, create, and grow.

Our Vision

To become one of Kenya's most trusted and innovative providers of electronics, technology, creative, and business solutions.

Our Two Divisions

Lorraine Electronics Enterprise operates through two complementary divisions that work together to provide a broader range of products and services.

Lorraine Electronics

Focuses on electronics, technology products, accessories, and professional repair services. We help customers find suitable technology products for their personal, educational, professional, and business needs.

Lorraine Enterprise

Focuses on business-oriented solutions, creative services, IT support, and professional services designed to help entrepreneurs, professionals, institutions, and organizations establish, operate, and grow.
